## 5. Dual clinical-write security review — BOTH AGENTS: VERDICT: FAIL

Two independent agents reviewed the clinical-write surface (contract SAFETY L47-52 + L40 ABAC). Overlapping findings (both agents):

1. **MRN/two-identifier not enforced on every encounter-scoped write.** `requireMrn` (`EncounterService.kt:583-591`) is called only on `placeOrder` (:321) and `prescribe` (:389). Note sign, vitals, and the other write paths skip it — SAFETY L48's two-identifier confirmation holds only for orders/Rx today.
2. **Empty allergy list passes silently as `allergyStatus='checked'`; no NKA sentinel.** SAFETY L49 requires an *explicit, audited* "no known allergies" record; grep for an NKA sentinel: 0 hits. A patient with a never-filled allergy list is indistinguishable from a verified-NKA patient at eRx time.
3. **Allergy severity semantics are string-shape, not clinical data.** `Allergy.severity` (`Allergy.kt:19-20`) is a free string that is never consulted; BLOCK/WARN are decided by lowercased substring matching (≥4 chars either direction). SAFETY L50's BLOCK/WARN contract is implemented on reaction-text shape.
4. **Note amendment mechanism missing.** Immutability itself **HOLDS** (no PUT/PATCH on notes; re-sign returns 409 `NOTE_ALREADY_SIGNED` — verified), but SAFETY L51's "amendments = new version with link" mechanism does not exist; a corrected note cannot be linked to its legal predecessor.
5. **ABAC is RBAC-only — no encounter-owner check.** `SquamedPermissionEvaluator` is a role/permission string match; there is no owner predicate on encounter-scoped writes, and `StartEncounterDto.doctorId` is client-supplied. Contract L40's "own encounters only — ABAC on encounter owner" is not enforced.
6. **Additional findings:** `AuditRecorder` fails open (`AuditRecorder.kt:91-94`); completed encounters still accept writes; `removeDiagnosis` hard-deletes without guard; `cancelOrder` exists but is unexposed and unguarded (`:361-371`); `FhirService.allEncounters()` has no tenant filter (`FhirService.kt:386`).

**Disposition:** both FAIL verdicts are recorded verbatim here and in `FINAL.md`'s safety register as the chunk-1 owed-hardening list. The exit demo runs on the gates that *are* implemented (BLOCK/WARN, immutability, role boundaries) — those work; the FAIL verdicts are about the safety items above, which are internal (no external dependency) and are queued as the top of chunk-2's remediation lane, not silently absorbed into this docs-only section's scope. No clinical sign-off is claimed: the sign-off block in FINAL.md remains pending. **Update 2026-11-18:** the six items LANDED in hardening PR #63 (squash 65fd4bd, run 44334006132) — §7 below; the FAIL verdicts above stay verbatim as the historical record at review time; clinical sign-off (B2) remains the owner's human gate.
